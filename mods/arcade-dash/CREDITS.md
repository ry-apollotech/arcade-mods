# Credits

## Pikachu (default pet)

`hooks/pets/pikachu.ts` is fan art of Pikachu, converted from pixel-art images found online. Pikachu is a character owned by Nintendo / Game Freak / The Pokémon Company.
This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. If you are the artist of the source sprites
or a rights holder and want them credited differently or removed, open an issue.

To use your own pet instead, drop `walk.gif` and `rest.gif` into `~/.claude/arcade-pet/` (see the README).
